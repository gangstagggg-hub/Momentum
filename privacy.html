Momentum – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Momentum is a personal workout log. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Summary

Momentum does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

Information you enter

The app lets you store exercises, weights, reps, comments, optional photos, weekly muscle training counts, body weight and body fat entries, workout programs, and your appearance and timer settings. In the Android app this information is saved in the app's own private storage on your device. It is separate from your web browser, it is never uploaded to a server, and the developer cannot see it. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage in Android settings. Momentum turns off Android's automatic cloud backup for its data, so it stays only on your phone.

Accounts, analytics and advertising

  • Momentum has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • Momentum contains no analytics or tracking tools.
  • Momentum shows no advertising.
  • Your information is not shared with or sold to anyone.

Fonts and other websites

The Android app contains its own fonts and does not load anything from other websites. The web version of Momentum, when opened in a browser, loads fonts from Google Fonts and Fontshare, which may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, as with any website that loads web fonts. Momentum does not send them any of the information you enter.

Camera and photos

If you choose to attach a photo to an exercise, the app asks to use your camera or photo gallery. The photo is stored locally on your device together with that exercise and is not uploaded anywhere.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children

Momentum is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
